How it works

1

Learn

Free 10-minute lessons that work on a phone and use little data. Graphic design, data entry, social media, basic coding.

Local businesses post small paid tasks. Pass a skill quiz to unlock the tasks for that skill, then get paid in naira when the business approves your work.

3

Grow

Your profile shows your skills, ratings and finished work. Help learners to earn tutoring fees and badges.
